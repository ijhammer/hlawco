Who is Yitzy?

Yitzy Hammer is the founder of Hammer & Co. Law Offices. He's been working with founders and entrepreneurs since 2013, and in legal practice since 2015, advising hi-tech and emerging technology companies — including deep, hands-on experience in blockchain and crypto.

His practice covers the full lifecycle of a growing company — formation, fundraising, commercial and employment agreements, regulatory compliance, and exit — for founders across hi-tech, and token issuance and regulatory work for those building in crypto.

"We are more than just your lawyers. We are your partners, strategists, and friends."

Yitzy is featured in major publications and speaks at conferences from Paris to Miami to Singapore, including as a strategic advisor and investor in select blockchain and Web3 initiatives.

More than a lawyer.

Beyond his legal practice, Yitzy serves as a Captain (Reserve) in the International Law Department of the IDF's Military Advocate General's Corps — bringing a unique perspective on international law and compliance to his work.

He is CIPP/E certified, meaning his data privacy and GDPR advice is grounded in formal, tested expertise.

As host of Beyond the Code — now with 100+ episodes — Yitzy has interviewed regulators, founders, and policymakers from across the globe, including SEC Commissioner Hester Peirce. He's also active as a strategic advisor and investor in select crypto and Web3 projects.
