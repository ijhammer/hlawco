Who is Yitzy?

Yitzy Hammer is the founder of Hammer & Co. Law Offices. With nearly a decade of experience advising crypto-native companies, he has become one of the most recognized legal voices in the blockchain space.

His practice covers the full lifecycle of a Web3 company — from formation and fundraising, through token issuance and regulatory compliance, to exit and M&A. He brings the technical fluency and legal precision that founders in this space demand.

"We are more than just your lawyers. We are your partners, strategists, and friends."

Yitzy is featured in major publications, speaks at conferences from Paris to Miami to Singapore, and is involved in NFT projects, DAOs, and Web3 initiatives as a strategic advisor and investor.

More than a lawyer.

Beyond his legal practice, Yitzy serves as a Captain (Reserve) in the International Law Department of the IDF's Military Advocate General's Corps — bringing a unique perspective on international law and compliance to his work.

He is CIPP/E certified, meaning his data privacy and GDPR advice is grounded in formal, tested expertise.

As host of Beyond the Code — now with 100+ episodes — Yitzy has interviewed regulators, founders, and policymakers from across the globe, including SEC Commissioner Hester Peirce.

Yitzy participates in multiple NFT projects, DAOs, and Web3 initiatives as a strategic advisor and investor — meaning he understands your world not just as a lawyer, but as a participant with skin in the game.
