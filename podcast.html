On Beyond the Code, Yitzy Hammer goes backstage with the founders, regulators, and legal minds shaping the frontier of crypto and emerging tech.

Each conversation digs into two things: the origin story that got them here, and the hard questions no one has clean answers to yet — where the technology is actually heading, who's writing the rules, and what it takes to build responsibly when the ground keeps shifting.

Over 100 episodes in, it's become a home for the people actually building, regulating, and enforcing the future of digital assets.
