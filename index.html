Yitzy has been working with founders and entrepreneurs since 2013, and in legal practice since 2015. His focus is hi-tech and emerging technology — with deep, hands-on experience in blockchain and crypto, where he's also an investor and advisor, not just outside counsel.

His practice covers the full lifecycle: formation, fundraising, commercial and employment agreements, regulatory compliance, and exit — plus token issuance for clients building in crypto. He also hosts Beyond the Code, one of the longest-running podcasts on crypto law, with 100+ episodes.
